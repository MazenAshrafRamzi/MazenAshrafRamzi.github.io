What the well remembers

  1. May 2026 – now

    Unity Developer · Al Motahida Education

    2D educational mini-games built into digital eBooks for the Ministry of Education, Sultanate of Oman: 15+ exercises with drag-and-drop, sorting, multi-select, gestures and canvas drawing. WebGL optimization, Arabic RTL text, an event-driven sequence architecture and state persistence.

    C# · Unity · DOTween · Odin Inspector · WebGL · Git

  2. Jan – May 2026

    Unity Developer · 7P Software and Marketing

    An automotive testing training simulator: a multimeter and a tester with about 30 connection points that react to real component values, plus a tutorial level, authentication and progress-gated levels. 10+ assets made with AI generation and photogrammetry.

    C# · Unity · Blender · Tripo3D · RealityScan

  3. 2020 – 2025

    B.Sc. Computer Science & Engineering · German University in Cairo

    Bachelor project turned engineering drawings into XR-ready 3D models with computer vision and Blender. Published at PAAMS 2025 (Springer CCIS).
